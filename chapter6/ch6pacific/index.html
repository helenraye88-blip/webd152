Welcome to Pacific Trails Resort

Nestled along the stunning Northern California coast, Pacific Trails Resort offers a one-of-a-kind escape into nature. Our resort blends rustic charm with modern comfort, giving you the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation.

Whether you're looking to hike along breathtaking coastal trails, unwind in a cozy yurt under the stars, or simply breathe in the fresh ocean air — Pacific Trails Resort has something for everyone.

Our Location

Located just minutes from the rugged Pacific coastline, our resort is surrounded by ancient redwood forests and pristine beaches. The natural beauty of the region provides a stunning backdrop for every activity and every moment of rest.
