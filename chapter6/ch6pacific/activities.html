Outdoor Activities

Pacific Trails Resort is the perfect base camp for outdoor adventure. From gentle coastal walks to challenging mountain hikes, there is an activity for every fitness level and every sense of adventure.

Hiking Trails

Explore miles of scenic trails winding through old-growth redwoods, along dramatic sea cliffs, and through wildflower meadows. Trail maps are available at the front desk, and guided hikes are offered every weekend morning.

Water Activities

The nearby Pacific Ocean offers outstanding opportunities for kayaking, surfing, and tide pool exploration. Equipment rentals and lessons are available through our activity center.

Wildlife Watching

The Central Coast is home to an incredible variety of wildlife. Keep your eyes open for gray whales, harbor seals, sea otters, and hundreds of bird species throughout the year.
