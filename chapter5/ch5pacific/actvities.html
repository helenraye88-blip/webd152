Activities at Pacific Trails

Hiking

Pacific Trails Resort has 5 miles of hiking trails and is adjacent to a state park. Go it alone or join one of our guided hikes.

Kayaking

Ocean kayaks are available for guest use. Explore the Pacific coastline and enjoy the beautiful scenery from the water.

Bird Watching

Bring your binoculars and enjoy the many birds that live near the resort. Our staff can recommend the best viewing areas.
