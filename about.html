Our Story

How It All Began

Pacific Trails Resort was born from a simple but powerful idea: that everyone deserves a place to slow down, breathe in the salt air, and reconnect with the natural world. Founded in 2008 by Helen Hernandez, a lifelong lover of the California coast, the resort began as just two yurts on a quiet bluff overlooking the Pacific Ocean.

Helen grew up in Big Sur, spending summers hiking the coastal trails with her family. After years working in the hospitality industry, she returned home with a dream — to create a retreat that felt as wild and honest as the landscape itself. She wanted guests to experience the coast not from behind glass, but beneath open skies, along winding trails, and out on the open water.

Growing Into a Community

Over the years, Pacific Trails Resort grew from those original two yurts into the welcoming retreat it is today. We added kayak rentals, guided bird watching tours, sunrise yoga sessions, and a full reservations program — all while staying true to Helen's founding vision of simplicity, sustainability, and respect for the land.

Our small but dedicated team shares Helen's love of the outdoors and her commitment to making every guest feel at home. Whether you're a seasoned hiker or stepping onto a trail for the first time, we believe this coastline has something to offer you.

Our Promise to You

At Pacific Trails Resort, we promise to take care of this place so that generations to come can experience the same wonder that inspired Helen all those years ago. We keep our footprint small, our hospitality warm, and our trails well-marked. Come as a guest — leave as part of the family.

Helen Hernandez
