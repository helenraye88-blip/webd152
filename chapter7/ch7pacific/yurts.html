Yurt Accommodations

The Yurt Experience

Experience the magic of sleeping in one of our beautifully appointed yurts. Each yurt is thoughtfully designed to provide comfort while keeping you close to nature. Large skylight windows let you stargaze from the warmth of your bed.

Yurt Features

Our yurts come fully equipped with queen-size beds, cozy linens, small kitchenettes, and private decks overlooking the forest. Each unit has heating and electricity, ensuring a comfortable stay in any season.
