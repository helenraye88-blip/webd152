Yurts

Our private yurts offer a unique lodging experience surrounded by the natural beauty of the California North Coast. Each yurt at Pacific Trails Resort is sumptuously appointed and features a private deck with stunning ocean views.

Yurt Sizes
Our yurts range from cozy single-room retreats to spacious family yurts with separate sleeping areas.
Amenities
Each yurt includes a private deck, luxury linens, and stunning views of the Pacific Ocean.
Pet Policy
We welcome well-behaved pets in select yurts. Please contact us in advance to make arrangements.
