Activities

Pacific Trails Resort offers a variety of outdoor activities to make your stay unforgettable. Whether you seek adventure or relaxation, we have something for everyone along the beautiful California North Coast.

Hiking

Explore miles of scenic trails winding through coastal redwood forests and along dramatic ocean cliffs. Trails are available for all skill levels, from gentle nature walks to challenging ridge hikes with breathtaking views.

Kayaking

Paddle through the crystal-clear waters of the Pacific and explore hidden sea caves, rocky coves, and abundant marine life. Kayak rentals and guided tours are available for guests of all experience levels.

Bird Watching

The California North Coast is a haven for bird enthusiasts. Spot rare migratory species, nesting seabirds, and majestic raptors along our dedicated bird watching trails and observation platforms.
